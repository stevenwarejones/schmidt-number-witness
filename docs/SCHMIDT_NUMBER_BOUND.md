# Sharp Schmidt-number-two bound

Status: exact computer-assisted proof with an independently implemented verifier. A second AI system has reviewed it (`docs/review_2026-09-06_ai.md`); external human review and the novelty audit remain outstanding.

## Results

For the Bell functional F in THEORY.md, every behavior generated by a bipartite state of **Schmidt number at most two**, with three binary local POVMs per party and setting-independent shared randomness, satisfies

\[
\boxed{F\le7.}
\]

The bound is attained, so it is sharp. There is no residual epsilon in this statement.

The existing exactly specified qutrit construction has

\[
F(Q)=7.0129123854899715\ldots>7.
\]

Consequently, any violation F>7 certifies **Schmidt number at least three**, and hence local dimension at least three on both sides. The same violation also places the behavior outside the nonsignaling partial-local hull H, and therefore excludes every quantum compatible-pair mixture in C, by the previously verified facet theorem.

These statements use the ordinary local quantum Bell-test model and setting independence. They neither assume nor imply a hidden signaling mechanism or observer-relative events. This is a mathematical certification criterion and an explicit quantum realization, not an experimental observation.

## 1. Exact sum-of-squares identity

For nontrivial projective qubit observables, write A_i=u_i.sigma and B_j=v_j.sigma, where the Bloch vectors are unit vectors. Then

\[
A_i^2=B_j^2=I,\quad
\{A_i,A_j\}=2g^A_{ij}I,\quad
\{B_i,B_j\}=2g^B_{ij}I,
\]

where the g variables are real scalar inner products. Operators on different parties commute.

The certificate contains:

- 84 operator words W_i, each of length at most three;
- an integer matrix P of size 84 by 70;
- a symmetric rational matrix X of size 70 by 70.

Define J_j=sum_i P_ij W_i. The verifier establishes **both**

\[
X\succ0
\]

and the exact polynomial identity

\[
\boxed{7I-\mathcal B_F=\sum_{j,k=1}^{70}X_{jk}J_j^\dagger J_k.}
\]

Positivity is verified by exact rational LDL pivots. The identity is checked by reducing every word using the qubit anticommutator identities and requiring all coefficients to agree exactly. There are **zero residual coefficients**.

Since X is positive definite, the right side is positive semidefinite for every choice of qubit observables. Taking its expectation in any two-qubit state proves F<=7 in the nontrivial projective case. The integer basis map need not be justified by the numerical discovery procedure: once the identity and positivity are verified, those two facts alone establish this operator bound.

The standalone verifier uses a right-insertion normal-order algorithm, separately implemented from the adjacent-swap algorithm used by the SDP constructor. It does not import the optimization code, an SDP solver, NumPy, SymPy, or FLINT.

## 2. Arbitrary binary qubit POVMs

A binary effect lies in the compact convex set 0<=M<=I, whose extreme points are projections. For fixed state and other measurements the Bell functional is affine in each effect. Therefore a maximum over all six effects can be attained with each effect projective.

A projective qubit observable is either nontrivial and traceless, or deterministic (+I or -I). If any observable is deterministic, it is jointly measurable with another measurement on that party. The behavior then belongs to one of the partial-local classes. The separately supplied exact facet-validity certificate gives F<=7 in that case.

This covers all projective cases, and hence all binary qubit POVMs. The state may be mixed.

## 3. Schmidt-number extension

Every pure state of Schmidt rank at most two is supported on local subspaces of dimensions at most two. Compressing the local binary POVMs to those subspaces preserves the probabilities and yields valid qubit POVMs. Thus the bound holds for any such pure state, even in larger ambient Hilbert spaces.

For a state of Schmidt number at most two, apply this argument to a decomposition into pure states of Schmidt rank at most two and average. Linearity also permits setting-independent mixtures of measurement implementations.

This proves the stated theorem for all Schmidt-number-two models, not merely for systems explicitly built from two qubits.

## 4. Sharpness and qutrit separation

Take the product state |00>, all three Alice observables equal to Z, and all three Bob observables equal to -Z. Then A_i=+1, B_j=-1 and E_ij=-1 in expectation, giving F=7 exactly.

The previously shipped qutrit state and POVMs are reconstructed from exact rational/Gaussian-integer data by `proofs/verify_routing.py`. Its exact F value is strictly larger than 7. The main check runner also verifies that the Bell coefficient vectors match across the facet, sharp-bound, and quantum-realization certificates.

## 5. A quantitative consequence

A second, dimension-unrestricted certificate proves

\[
F\le U:=\frac{7041387041}{10^9}=7.041387041
\]

for arbitrary local quantum binary measurements. It uses only A_i^2=B_j^2=I and commutation between parties, with no qubit anticommutator identities. Its positive rational Gram matrix and an explicitly bounded residual are checked exactly. Binary POVMs are included by the same extremal-effect argument. This upper bound U is certified but not claimed sharp.

Suppose a behavior admits a decomposition

\[
P=(1-t)P_2+tP_{\rm Q},
\]

where P_2 is realizable with Schmidt number at most two and P_Q is any quantum behavior. Then

\[
F(P)\le7(1-t)+Ut,
\quad\text{so}\quad
\boxed{t\ge\frac{F(P)-7}{U-7}}\qquad(F(P)>7).
\]

For the exact qutrit example, this lower bound is

\[
t\ge0.3119910285\ldots>31.19\%.
\]

This is a lower bound on the unrestricted remainder in **every such decomposition**, not a claimed exact minimum. Applied to a pure-state decomposition of an underlying state, at least that much weight must be carried by Schmidt-rank-at-least-three components. It is not a statement that particular observed runs carry accessible labels identifying their entanglement dimension.

## 7. What remains open

The **sharp qubit/Schmidt-number-two bound is no longer conjectural in this project**. What remains to be established independently is the correctness of this proof package and its novelty relative to existing dimension-witness and incompatibility literature.

The global quantum maximum of F is not determined here. Nor is the decomposition-weight bound claimed tight, the full two-sided polytope classified, or experimental feasibility established.

Dimension-constrained SDP methods are established, for example in [Navascues and Vertesi (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.0924) and [Navascues et al. (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07521). The contribution to audit is this particular sharp witness bound, its two-sided facet role, and the joint physical conclusion—not the general idea of dimension certification.
