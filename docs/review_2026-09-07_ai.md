No defect was found that overturns the sharp Schmidt-number-two bound, the qutrit separation,
the 13/13/14 face dimensions, or the quantum-remainder lower bound. That conclusion rests on
independent reconstruction, not on rerunning this repository's code: the reviewer built exact
local hidden-variable models for all 48 one-sided face points and all 15 two-sided facet
points, recomputed their affine ranks, and rebuilt all 36 qutrit probabilities from full 9×9

## What it found, and what was done

**Two demonstrated false positives in `tests/test_research_paths.py`.** Its namespace rebuild
used `exec` over top-level assignments, so an assignment whose right-hand side contains a call
— `probe = (OUTPUT_DIR / 'x.txt').write_text(...)` — made the *test* perform the write,
contradicting its own claim that no code was executed. Separately, its resolver dropped the
right operand of a division on `NameError` until some prefix resolved, discarding later `..`
segments, so `OUTPUT_DIR / dynamic / '..' / '..' / 'escaped.txt'` was approved on the strength
of its `build/` prefix while normalizing outside `build/`. Both were reproduced and are now
regression tests; the resolver executes nothing and rejects traversal and unknown directory
components.

**Prior-art corrections**, all applied: a numerical lower bound was quoted as if it were a
maximum; the heading "no tight binary 3322 inequality is a dimension witness" was false as
stated, since I3322 *is* one in the qubit-versus-arbitrary-dimension sense and only fails to be
a qubit-versus-**qutrit** witness; "slack" was the wrong word for a parent inequality that
*exceeds* its bound outside its own one-sided domain; "Alice-side operations" should be
"relabelings without party exchange"; the printed correlator index disagreed with the
repository's declared zero-based convention; and a local-bound check was described as
authenticating a transcription, which it cannot do, since many coefficient vectors share a
local bound.

**Constructive proofs supplied**, now incorporated: the binary-POVM and Schmidt-compression
reduction of `docs/SCHMIDT_NUMBER_BOUND.md` §2–3, and an exact qubit achiever for I3322 —
`Φ⁺` with `A₀=B₀=(√3X+Z)/2`, `A₁=B₁=(√3X−Z)/2`, `A₂=B₂=Z`, scoring exactly 5 — which removed
the last literature dependency from `proofs/verify_novelty_comparison.py`.

**Primary-source grounding** for the detection-efficiency comparison: Brunner, Gisin, Scarani
and Simon, [quant-ph/0702130](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0702130), give the probability
polynomial and detection formula from which the one-sided expression is proportional to
`J + ((1−η)/η)·[p_A(0|0) − 1]` — a marginal plus a constant. The repository's
correlator-support obstruction therefore applies to that construction on a primary source
rather than a search summary. This does not extend to setting-dependent efficiencies or
postselection.

## On the completeness question

The reviewer confirmed that positivity plus all CHSH restrictions is complete for binary 2×3
locality, by Fine's theorem and an interval-intersection argument, and made a sharper point:
**the upper-bound duals need only necessary inequalities.** Positive combinations of positivity
and CHSH already prove validity on local restrictions. Completeness matters when those rows are
used as a membership test, not when used to prove an upper bound.

## Recommended next calculation

Study the probability-conditioned qubit tradeoff `M_A <= 6 + 4·p(00|10)` for Schmidt number at
most two, and ask whether 4 is the smallest universally valid coefficient:

    alpha* = sup over P in Q_2 with p(00|10) > 0 of  (M_A(P) - 6) / p(00|10).

The present theorem proves `alpha* <= 4`. It does **not** prove equality merely because
`F <= 7` is sharp — a saturation point with `p = 0` gives no lower bound on that ratio. This is
a bounded question that could turn a single functional into a sharp tradeoff statement. It is
not a result.

## Limits of this review
